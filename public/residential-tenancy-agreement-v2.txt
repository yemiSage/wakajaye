RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT

This Residential Tenancy Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement") is made and entered into on this ______ day of __________, 20, between the Landlord or authorised Caretaker (hereinafter referred to as the "Landlord") and the undersigned Tenant(s) (hereinafter collectively referred to as the "Occupants").

By signing this Agreement, the Occupants acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agreed to comply with the terms and conditions governing their occupation of the premises.

1. LIMITATION OF OCCUPANTS

1.1. Big Rooms: Rooms designated as "Big Rooms" shall accommodate a maximum of two (2) occupants at any given time.

1.2. Small Rooms: Rooms designated as "Small Rooms" shall accommodate a maximum of one (1) occupant at any given time.

1.3. Occupants shall not exceed the approved number of persons assigned to their respective rooms without prior written consent from the Landlord.

1.4. Subletting, transferring occupancy rights, or accommodating additional permanent residents without the Landlord's approval is strictly prohibited.

2. ANNUAL RENT AND ADDITIONAL CHARGES

2.1. All Occupants shall be required to pay their annual rent together with the applicable electricity and cleaning contributions.

2.2. Big Room Payment Breakdown

Annual Room Rent: ₦200,000

Annual Electricity Contribution: ₦42,000

Annual Cleaning Contribution: ₦12,000

Total Annual Payment: ₦254,000 (Two Hundred and Fifty-Four Thousand Naira Only).

2.3. Small Room Payment Breakdown

Annual Room Rent: ₦150,000

Annual Electricity Contribution: ₦42,000

Annual Cleaning Contribution: ₦12,000

Total Annual Payment: ₦204,000 (Two Hundred and Four Thousand Naira Only).

2.4. The electricity and cleaning contributions cover a period of twelve (12) months and shall be paid alongside the annual rent.

2.5. The amounts stated above represent the total annual payments applicable to each room category and shall be paid in full before the commencement or renewal of tenancy, unless otherwise agreed in writing by the Landlord.

3. CONTRIBUTIONS FOR HOUSE MAINTENANCE

3.1. All Occupants shall be responsible for maintaining a clean, safe, peaceful, and habitable living environment within the premises.

3.2. The annual cleaning contribution shall be used towards the regular cleaning and upkeep of shared facilities and common areas within the property.

3.3. Where additional maintenance or repairs become necessary, Occupants may be required to make reasonable contributions, provided that such expenses are clearly communicated and agreed upon in advance.

3.4. Occupants shall be responsible for the cost of repairing or replacing any property, equipment, or facility damaged through their negligence, misuse, or deliberate actions, excluding normal wear and tear.

3.5. Failure to comply with agreed maintenance obligations may constitute a breach of this Agreement and may result in eviction through lawful proceedings.

4. TENANCY DURATION AND RENEWAL

4.1. The tenancy shall run for a period of twelve (12) months, commencing from the agreed tenancy start date.

4.2. Occupants wishing to renew their tenancy shall communicate their intention to the Landlord before the expiration of their existing tenancy.

4.3. Renewal of tenancy shall be subject to the payment of applicable rent and charges and continued compliance with the terms of this Agreement.

4.4. Any proposed change to rent or other charges for a subsequent tenancy period shall be communicated in advance and shall be subject to applicable law.

5.2. Annual rent and associated charges shall be paid on or before the agreed commencement or renewal date of the tenancy, unless a different payment arrangement has been approved by the Landlord.

5.3. All payments made by Occupants shall be acknowledged through a payment receipt or other acceptable proof of payment.

5.4. Late Payment: Where rent remains unpaid for two (2) calendar months after its due date, a late payment fee of ₦20,000 (Twenty Thousand Naira Only) shall be imposed, subject to applicable law.

5.5. Failure to pay outstanding rent or other agreed financial obligations may result in the commencement of lawful tenancy termination or debt recovery procedures.

6. HOUSE RULES AND OCCUPANT CONDUCT

6.1. All Occupants shall conduct themselves responsibly and maintain peaceful relationships with fellow residents and neighbouring properties.

6.2. Fighting, theft, threats, harassment, deliberate property damage, and activities that threaten the safety or peaceful enjoyment of the premises are strictly prohibited.

6.3. Occupants shall not engage in illegal activities within the property or use the premises for any unlawful purpose.

6.4. Excessive noise, persistent disturbances, or activities that inconvenience other residents shall not be permitted.

6.5. Shared facilities, including bathrooms, toilets, kitchens, passageways, and other common areas, must be properly maintained and kept clean after use.

6.6. Occupants shall not make structural alterations, install permanent fixtures, or undertake major modifications without prior approval from the Landlord.

6.7. Repeated violation of these house rules may result in disciplinary action, formal notice, or termination proceedings in accordance with applicable law.

7. TERMINATION OF TENANCY AND QUIT PREMISES RULE

7.1. Late Payment: Where rent remains unpaid or tenancy is not renewed for a continuous period of two (2) months after its due date, the Landlord may initiate the appropriate legal process for recovery of the premises.

7.2. Misconduct or Illegal Activities: Any serious misconduct, including theft, intentional property damage, violence, threats, persistent disturbance of peace, or illegal activities, shall constitute grounds for the Landlord to seek termination of the tenancy, subject to applicable law.

7.3. Breach of Agreement: Failure to comply with any material provision of this Agreement may result in termination proceedings after the appropriate notice has been issued in accordance with the law.

7.4. Notice to Quit: Where a notice to quit or notice of intention to recover possession is required, the Landlord shall issue the appropriate notice for the tenancy and circumstances, in accordance with the law applicable to the location of the property.

7.5. Failure to Vacate: Where an Occupant fails to vacate after the expiration of a valid notice, the Landlord reserves the right to pursue lawful recovery of possession through the appropriate court or authority.

7.6. The Landlord shall not forcibly evict an Occupant, unlawfully seize or remove personal belongings, or otherwise recover possession without following the required legal procedures.

7.7. Upon lawful termination or expiration of tenancy, Occupants shall return the premises in reasonable condition, excluding normal wear and tear, and settle all outstanding financial obligations.

8. GENERAL CONDITIONS

8.1. This Agreement shall be binding upon the Landlord and all Occupants who sign it.

8.2. All Occupants shall be responsible for complying with the rules and financial obligations applicable to their respective rooms.

8.3. Any amendment or modification to this Agreement shall be communicated and agreed upon in writing by the parties.

8.4. Any provision of this Agreement found to be inconsistent with applicable law shall be interpreted or adjusted to comply with that law without automatically invalidating the remaining provisions.

8.5. This Agreement shall be governed by the applicable laws of the state in which the property is located.

9. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT AND ACCEPTANCE

By signing below, the Landlord and Occupants confirm that they have read, understood, and voluntarily agreed to the terms and conditions contained in this Residential Tenancy Agreement.

The Occupants further acknowledge their responsibility to comply with the payment obligations, occupancy limits, maintenance responsibilities, and house rules throughout the tenancy period.
